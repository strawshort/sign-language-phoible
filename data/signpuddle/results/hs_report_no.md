# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Norway has a total of 6491 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 6148 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 16053 total handshape tokens, of
which 112 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 3553 instances (making up 22.13% of all tokens) over
1810 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.43%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 4 handshapes in the dataset. These top 4 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (3553 tokens ~ 22.13%, 1810 glosses)
2. **S100** (2198 tokens ~ 13.69%, 1137 glosses)
3. **S14c** (1806 tokens ~ 11.25%, 882 glosses)
4. **S177** (538 tokens ~ 3.35%, 320 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 1810
glosses (27.88% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 4
handshapes that cover the most glosses, along with the number and
percent of glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (1810 glosses ~ 27.88% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (1137 glosses ~ 17.52% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (882 glosses ~ 13.59% of all glosses)
4. **S177** (320 glosses ~ 4.93% of all glosses)
