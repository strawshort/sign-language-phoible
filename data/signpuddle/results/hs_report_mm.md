# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Myanmar has a total of 5 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 5 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 10 total handshape tokens, of which 6
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 3 instances (making up 30.0% of all tokens) over 2 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (70.0%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 3 handshapes in the dataset. These top 3 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (3 tokens ~ 30.0%, 2 glosses)
2. **S1f5** (2 tokens ~ 20.0%, 1 glosses)
3. **S1da** (2 tokens ~ 20.0%, 1 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 2 glosses
(40.0% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 3 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (2 glosses ~ 40.0% of all glosses)
2. **S1f5** (1 glosses ~ 20.0% of all glosses)
3. **S1da** (1 glosses ~ 20.0% of all glosses)
