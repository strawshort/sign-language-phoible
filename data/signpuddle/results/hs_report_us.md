# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for USA has a total of 11984 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 11247 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 37379 total handshape tokens, of
which 182 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 5528 instances (making up 14.79% of all tokens) over
2260 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.23%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 7 handshapes in the dataset. These top 7 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (5528 tokens ~ 14.79%, 2260 glosses)
2. **S100** (4049 tokens ~ 10.83%, 1793 glosses)
3. **S14c** (3437 tokens ~ 9.2%, 1381 glosses)
4. **S15d** (1568 tokens ~ 4.19%, 615 glosses)
5. **S16d** (1428 tokens ~ 3.82%, 629 glosses)
6. **S1f7** (1392 tokens ~ 3.72%, 635 glosses)
7. **S185** (1374 tokens ~ 3.68%, 575 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 2260
glosses (18.86% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 7
handshapes that cover the most glosses, along with the number and
percent of glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (2260 glosses ~ 18.86% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (1793 glosses ~ 14.96% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (1381 glosses ~ 11.52% of all glosses)
4. **S1f7** (635 glosses ~ 5.3% of all glosses)
5. **S16d** (629 glosses ~ 5.25% of all glosses)
6. **S15d** (615 glosses ~ 5.13% of all glosses)
7. **S185** (575 glosses ~ 4.8% of all glosses)
