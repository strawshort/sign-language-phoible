# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Italy has a total of 1183 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1008 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 1694 total handshape tokens, of
which 89 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S14c
with a total of 251 instances (making up 14.82% of all tokens) over 175
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.48%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 8 handshapes in the dataset. These top 8 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S14c** (251 tokens ~ 14.82%, 175 glosses)
2. **S100** (148 tokens ~ 8.74%, 117 glosses)
3. **S15d** (138 tokens ~ 8.15%, 103 glosses)
4. **S10e** (111 tokens ~ 6.55%, 71 glosses)
5. **S1dc** (60 tokens ~ 3.54%, 43 glosses)
6. **S147** (57 tokens ~ 3.36%, 42 glosses)
7. **S185** (54 tokens ~ 3.19%, 36 glosses)
8. **S10b** (53 tokens ~ 3.13%, 45 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S14c, appearing in 175 glosses
(14.79% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 8 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S14c** (175 glosses ~ 14.79% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (117 glosses ~ 9.89% of all glosses)
3. **S15d** (103 glosses ~ 8.71% of all glosses)
4. **S10e** (71 glosses ~ 6.0% of all glosses)
5. **S10b** (45 glosses ~ 3.8% of all glosses)
6. **S1dc** (43 glosses ~ 3.63% of all glosses)
7. **S147** (42 glosses ~ 3.55% of all glosses)
8. **S11e** (39 glosses ~ 3.3% of all glosses)
