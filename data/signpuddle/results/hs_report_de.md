# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Germany has a total of 1500 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1400 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 3297 total handshape tokens, of
which 97 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15d
with a total of 544 instances (making up 16.5% of all tokens) over 323
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (54.05%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (544 tokens ~ 16.5%, 323 glosses)
2. **S14c** (443 tokens ~ 13.44%, 229 glosses)
3. **S100** (338 tokens ~ 10.25%, 222 glosses)
4. **S1ce** (161 tokens ~ 4.88%, 83 glosses)
5. **S15a** (157 tokens ~ 4.76%, 90 glosses)
6. **S10e** (139 tokens ~ 4.22%, 71 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 323 glosses
(21.53% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (323 glosses ~ 21.53% of all glosses)
2. **S14c** (229 glosses ~ 15.27% of all glosses)
3. **S100** (222 glosses ~ 14.8% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (90 glosses ~ 6.0% of all glosses)
5. **S1ce** (83 glosses ~ 5.53% of all glosses)
6. **S185** (82 glosses ~ 5.47% of all glosses)
