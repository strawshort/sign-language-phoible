# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Singapore has a total of 1122 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1090 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 2115 total handshape tokens, of
which 91 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 257 instances (making up 12.15% of all tokens) over 164
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.77%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 8 handshapes in the dataset. These top 8 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (257 tokens ~ 12.15%, 164 glosses)
2. **S100** (200 tokens ~ 9.46%, 139 glosses)
3. **S14c** (197 tokens ~ 9.31%, 129 glosses)
4. **S15d** (171 tokens ~ 8.09%, 114 glosses)
5. **S1f7** (86 tokens ~ 4.07%, 63 glosses)
6. **S115** (69 tokens ~ 3.26%, 44 glosses)
7. **S1f5** (59 tokens ~ 2.79%, 39 glosses)
8. **S16d** (56 tokens ~ 2.65%, 40 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 164 glosses
(14.62% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 8 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (164 glosses ~ 14.62% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (139 glosses ~ 12.39% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (129 glosses ~ 11.5% of all glosses)
4. **S15d** (114 glosses ~ 10.16% of all glosses)
5. **S1f7** (63 glosses ~ 5.61% of all glosses)
6. **S115** (44 glosses ~ 3.92% of all glosses)
7. **S16d** (40 glosses ~ 3.57% of all glosses)
8. **S1f5** (39 glosses ~ 3.48% of all glosses)
