# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Peru has a total of 146 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 139 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 201 total handshape tokens, of which
43 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S100 with a
total of 27 instances (making up 13.43% of all tokens) over 20 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (52.24%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 7 handshapes in the dataset. These top 7 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S100** (27 tokens ~ 13.43%, 20 glosses)
2. **S15d** (19 tokens ~ 9.45%, 15 glosses)
3. **S15a** (17 tokens ~ 8.46%, 10 glosses)
4. **S14c** (14 tokens ~ 6.97%, 11 glosses)
5. **S10e** (12 tokens ~ 5.97%, 12 glosses)
6. **S1f5** (9 tokens ~ 4.48%, 9 glosses)
7. **S1ed** (7 tokens ~ 3.48%, 5 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S100, appearing in 20 glosses
(13.7% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 7 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S100** (20 glosses ~ 13.7% of all glosses)
2. **S15d** (15 glosses ~ 10.27% of all glosses)
3. **S10e** (12 glosses ~ 8.22% of all glosses)
4. **S14c** (11 glosses ~ 7.53% of all glosses)
5. **S15a** (10 glosses ~ 6.85% of all glosses)
6. **S1f5** (9 glosses ~ 6.16% of all glosses)
7. **S176** (6 glosses ~ 4.11% of all glosses)

## Exclusions
