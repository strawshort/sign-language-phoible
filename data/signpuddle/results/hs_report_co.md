# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Colombia has a total of 1234 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1206 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 4261 total handshape tokens, of
which 129 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15d
with a total of 393 instances (making up 9.22% of all tokens) over 176
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (52.31%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 11 handshapes in the dataset. These top 11 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (393 tokens ~ 9.22%, 176 glosses)
2. **S100** (359 tokens ~ 8.43%, 163 glosses)
3. **S14c** (300 tokens ~ 7.04%, 117 glosses)
4. **S15a** (249 tokens ~ 5.84%, 112 glosses)
5. **S1ed** (176 tokens ~ 4.13%, 69 glosses)
6. **S10e** (156 tokens ~ 3.66%, 70 glosses)
7. **S185** (137 tokens ~ 3.22%, 59 glosses)
8. **S16d** (129 tokens ~ 3.03%, 64 glosses)
9. **S1f7** (116 tokens ~ 2.72%, 60 glosses)
10. **S1dc** (112 tokens ~ 2.63%, 55 glosses)
11. **S115** (102 tokens ~ 2.39%, 44 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 176 glosses
(14.26% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 11 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (176 glosses ~ 14.26% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (163 glosses ~ 13.21% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (117 glosses ~ 9.48% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (112 glosses ~ 9.08% of all glosses)
5. **S10e** (70 glosses ~ 5.67% of all glosses)
6. **S1ed** (69 glosses ~ 5.59% of all glosses)
