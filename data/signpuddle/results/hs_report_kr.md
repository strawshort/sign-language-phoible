# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for South Korea has a total of 57672 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 55499 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 125309 total handshape
tokens, of which 214 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15d with a total of 25904 instances (making up 20.67% of
all tokens) over 16603 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.52%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (25904 tokens ~ 20.67%, 16603 glosses)
2. **S100** (13302 tokens ~ 10.62%, 10234 glosses)
3. **S14c** (8347 tokens ~ 6.66%, 5505 glosses)
4. **S1f5** (6914 tokens ~ 5.52%, 5357 glosses)
5. **S1ce** (4802 tokens ~ 3.83%, 3671 glosses)
6. **S10a** (4034 tokens ~ 3.22%, 3284 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 16603
glosses (28.79% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6
handshapes that cover the most glosses, along with the number and
percent of glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (16603 glosses ~ 28.79% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (10234 glosses ~ 17.75% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (5505 glosses ~ 9.55% of all glosses)
4. **S1f5** (5357 glosses ~ 9.29% of all glosses)
5. **S1ce** (3671 glosses ~ 6.37% of all glosses)
6. **S10a** (3284 glosses ~ 5.69% of all glosses)
