# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Slovenia has a total of 3789 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 3609 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 7308 total handshape tokens, of
which 111 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 1301 instances (making up 17.8% of all tokens) over 800
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (53.65%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 7 handshapes in the dataset. These top 7 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (1301 tokens ~ 17.8%, 800 glosses)
2. **S100** (643 tokens ~ 8.8%, 468 glosses)
3. **S14c** (556 tokens ~ 7.61%, 364 glosses)
4. **S115** (445 tokens ~ 6.09%, 285 glosses)
5. **S15d** (398 tokens ~ 5.45%, 269 glosses)
6. **S1ed** (297 tokens ~ 4.06%, 264 glosses)
7. **S185** (281 tokens ~ 3.85%, 188 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 800 glosses
(21.11% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 7 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (800 glosses ~ 21.11% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (468 glosses ~ 12.35% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (364 glosses ~ 9.61% of all glosses)
4. **S115** (285 glosses ~ 7.52% of all glosses)
5. **S15d** (269 glosses ~ 7.1% of all glosses)
6. **S1ed** (264 glosses ~ 6.97% of all glosses)
7. **S1ea** (196 glosses ~ 5.17% of all glosses)
