# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Egypt has a total of 109 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 104 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 161 total handshape tokens, of which
39 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 38 instances (making up 23.6% of all tokens) over 28 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.55%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 5 handshapes in the dataset. These top 5 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (38 tokens ~ 23.6%, 28 glosses)
2. **S100** (18 tokens ~ 11.18%, 15 glosses)
3. **S14c** (10 tokens ~ 6.21%, 7 glosses)
4. **S1ed** (9 tokens ~ 5.59%, 7 glosses)
5. **S16d** (8 tokens ~ 4.97%, 6 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 28 glosses
(25.69% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 5 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (28 glosses ~ 25.69% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (15 glosses ~ 13.76% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (7 glosses ~ 6.42% of all glosses)
4. **S1ed** (7 glosses ~ 6.42% of all glosses)
5. **S16d** (6 glosses ~ 5.5% of all glosses)

## Exclusions

### Glosses Without Handshape

Of the 109 total glosses, 4.59% (5 glosses) did not include any
handshape symbol (i.e. no FSW code starting with S1xx) and thus were
excluded from the handshape frequency analysis. These glosses are listed
below.

- أوبرا (sid_79)
- جد (sid_41)
- حاول (sid_108)
- قناطر (sid_91)
- مظاهرة (sid_68)
