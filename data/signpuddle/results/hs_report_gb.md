# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Great Britain has a total of 841 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 767 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 1461 total handshape
tokens, of which 89 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15a with a total of 212 instances (making up 14.51% of all
tokens) over 132 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (52.22%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 5 handshapes in the dataset. These top 5 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (212 tokens ~ 14.51%, 132 glosses)
2. **S100** (206 tokens ~ 14.1%, 159 glosses)
3. **S14c** (180 tokens ~ 12.32%, 103 glosses)
4. **S15d** (97 tokens ~ 6.64%, 67 glosses)
5. **S115** (68 tokens ~ 4.65%, 38 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S100, appearing in 159 glosses
(18.91% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 5 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S100** (159 glosses ~ 18.91% of all glosses)
2. **S15a** (132 glosses ~ 15.7% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (103 glosses ~ 12.25% of all glosses)
4. **S15d** (67 glosses ~ 7.97% of all glosses)
5. **S115** (38 glosses ~ 4.52% of all glosses)
