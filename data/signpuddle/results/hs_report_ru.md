# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Russia has a total of 275 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 264 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 406 total handshape tokens, of which
57 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S14c with a
total of 51 instances (making up 12.56% of all tokens) over 41 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.97%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S14c** (51 tokens ~ 12.56%, 41 glosses)
2. **S15a** (42 tokens ~ 10.34%, 27 glosses)
3. **S15d** (40 tokens ~ 9.85%, 27 glosses)
4. **S100** (39 tokens ~ 9.61%, 29 glosses)
5. **S1ce** (21 tokens ~ 5.17%, 15 glosses)
6. **S115** (18 tokens ~ 4.43%, 13 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S14c, appearing in 41 glosses
(14.91% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S14c** (41 glosses ~ 14.91% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (29 glosses ~ 10.55% of all glosses)
3. **S15d** (27 glosses ~ 9.82% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (27 glosses ~ 9.82% of all glosses)
5. **S1ce** (15 glosses ~ 5.45% of all glosses)
6. **S115** (13 glosses ~ 4.73% of all glosses)
