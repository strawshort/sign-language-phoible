# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Finland has a total of 142 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 137 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 198 total handshape tokens, of which
40 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S100 with a
total of 36 instances (making up 18.18% of all tokens) over 30 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (53.54%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 4 handshapes in the dataset. These top 4 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S100** (36 tokens ~ 18.18%, 30 glosses)
2. **S15a** (26 tokens ~ 13.13%, 17 glosses)
3. **S14c** (24 tokens ~ 12.12%, 18 glosses)
4. **S15d** (20 tokens ~ 10.1%, 11 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S100, appearing in 30 glosses
(21.13% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 4 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S100** (30 glosses ~ 21.13% of all glosses)
2. **S14c** (18 glosses ~ 12.68% of all glosses)
3. **S15a** (17 glosses ~ 11.97% of all glosses)
4. **S15d** (11 glosses ~ 7.75% of all glosses)

## Exclusions

### Glosses Without Handshape

Of the 142 total glosses, 3.52% (5 glosses) did not include any
handshape symbol (i.e. no FSW code starting with S1xx) and thus were
excluded from the handshape frequency analysis. These glosses are listed
below.

- auto (sid_74)
- isoisa (Opa) (sid_84)
- moottoripyora (sid_143)
- s (sid_18)
- sunnuntai (sid_153)
