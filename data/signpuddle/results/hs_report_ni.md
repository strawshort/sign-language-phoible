# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Nicaragua has a total of 1707 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1592 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 3348 total handshape tokens, of
which 88 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 694 instances (making up 20.73% of all tokens) over 421
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.45%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (694 tokens ~ 20.73%, 421 glosses)
2. **S100** (279 tokens ~ 8.33%, 225 glosses)
3. **S14c** (236 tokens ~ 7.05%, 140 glosses)
4. **S16d** (170 tokens ~ 5.08%, 122 glosses)
5. **S15d** (168 tokens ~ 5.02%, 120 glosses)
6. **S1f7** (142 tokens ~ 4.24%, 99 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 421 glosses
(24.66% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (421 glosses ~ 24.66% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (225 glosses ~ 13.18% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (140 glosses ~ 8.2% of all glosses)
4. **S16d** (122 glosses ~ 7.15% of all glosses)
5. **S15d** (120 glosses ~ 7.03% of all glosses)
6. **S1f7** (99 glosses ~ 5.8% of all glosses)
