# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Jordan has a total of 20 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 17 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 33 total handshape tokens, of which 15
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S100 with a
total of 4 instances (making up 12.12% of all tokens) over 2 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (57.58%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S100** (4 tokens ~ 12.12%, 2 glosses)
2. **S182** (3 tokens ~ 9.09%, 3 glosses)
3. **S1f5** (3 tokens ~ 9.09%, 2 glosses)
4. **S15a** (3 tokens ~ 9.09%, 3 glosses)
5. **S150** (3 tokens ~ 9.09%, 2 glosses)
6. **S10e** (3 tokens ~ 9.09%, 2 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S182, appearing in 3 glosses
(15.0% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S182** (3 glosses ~ 15.0% of all glosses)
2. **S15a** (3 glosses ~ 15.0% of all glosses)
3. **S100** (2 glosses ~ 10.0% of all glosses)
4. **S1f5** (2 glosses ~ 10.0% of all glosses)
5. **S150** (2 glosses ~ 10.0% of all glosses)
6. **S10e** (2 glosses ~ 10.0% of all glosses)
