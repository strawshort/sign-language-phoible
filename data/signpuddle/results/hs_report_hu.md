# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Hungary has a total of 67 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 55 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 174 total handshape tokens, of which
20 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 48 instances (making up 27.59% of all tokens) over 16 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (57.47%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 3 handshapes in the dataset. These top 3 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (48 tokens ~ 27.59%, 16 glosses)
2. **S100** (30 tokens ~ 17.24%, 12 glosses)
3. **S14c** (22 tokens ~ 12.64%, 7 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 16 glosses
(23.88% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 3 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (16 glosses ~ 23.88% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (12 glosses ~ 17.91% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (7 glosses ~ 10.45% of all glosses)
