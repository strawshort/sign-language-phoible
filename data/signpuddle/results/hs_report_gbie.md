# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Northern Ireland has a total of 53 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 53 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 81 total handshape
tokens, of which 22 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15d with a total of 7 instances (making up 8.64% of all
tokens) over 4 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.62%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 7 handshapes in the dataset. These top 7 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (7 tokens ~ 8.64%, 4 glosses)
2. **S115** (6 tokens ~ 7.41%, 3 glosses)
3. **S118** (6 tokens ~ 7.41%, 3 glosses)
4. **S1f7** (6 tokens ~ 7.41%, 5 glosses)
5. **S1c6** (6 tokens ~ 7.41%, 3 glosses)
6. **S15a** (5 tokens ~ 6.17%, 4 glosses)
7. **S18e** (5 tokens ~ 6.17%, 5 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S1f7, appearing in 5 glosses
(9.43% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 7 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S18e** (5 glosses ~ 9.43% of all glosses)
2. **S1f7** (5 glosses ~ 9.43% of all glosses)
3. **S185** (5 glosses ~ 9.43% of all glosses)
4. **S15d** (4 glosses ~ 7.55% of all glosses)
5. **S176** (4 glosses ~ 7.55% of all glosses)
6. **S100** (4 glosses ~ 7.55% of all glosses)
7. **S11e** (4 glosses ~ 7.55% of all glosses)
