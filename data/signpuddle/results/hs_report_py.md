# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Paraguay has a total of 1840 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1761 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 3162 total handshape tokens, of
which 113 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S14c
with a total of 402 instances (making up 12.71% of all tokens) over 256
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.57%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S14c** (402 tokens ~ 12.71%, 256 glosses)
2. **S100** (363 tokens ~ 11.48%, 274 glosses)
3. **S15a** (321 tokens ~ 10.15%, 197 glosses)
4. **S15d** (313 tokens ~ 9.9%, 211 glosses)
5. **S10e** (116 tokens ~ 3.67%, 105 glosses)
6. **S115** (84 tokens ~ 2.66%, 59 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S100, appearing in 274 glosses
(14.89% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S100** (274 glosses ~ 14.89% of all glosses)
2. **S14c** (256 glosses ~ 13.91% of all glosses)
3. **S15d** (211 glosses ~ 11.47% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (197 glosses ~ 10.71% of all glosses)
5. **S10e** (105 glosses ~ 5.71% of all glosses)
6. **S176** (59 glosses ~ 3.21% of all glosses)
