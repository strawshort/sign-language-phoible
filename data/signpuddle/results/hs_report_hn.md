# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Honduras has a total of 3401 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 3242 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 10381 total handshape tokens, of
which 135 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S14c
with a total of 1057 instances (making up 10.18% of all tokens) over 451
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.95%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 9 handshapes in the dataset. These top 9 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S14c** (1057 tokens ~ 10.18%, 451 glosses)
2. **S15a** (915 tokens ~ 8.81%, 366 glosses)
3. **S100** (871 tokens ~ 8.39%, 496 glosses)
4. **S15d** (794 tokens ~ 7.65%, 354 glosses)
5. **S16d** (423 tokens ~ 4.07%, 236 glosses)
6. **S1f7** (402 tokens ~ 3.87%, 251 glosses)
7. **S10e** (345 tokens ~ 3.32%, 205 glosses)
8. **S176** (301 tokens ~ 2.9%, 193 glosses)
9. **S1ce** (285 tokens ~ 2.75%, 170 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S100, appearing in 496 glosses
(14.58% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 9 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S100** (496 glosses ~ 14.58% of all glosses)
2. **S14c** (451 glosses ~ 13.26% of all glosses)
3. **S15a** (366 glosses ~ 10.76% of all glosses)
4. **S15d** (354 glosses ~ 10.41% of all glosses)
5. **S1f7** (251 glosses ~ 7.38% of all glosses)
6. **S16d** (236 glosses ~ 6.94% of all glosses)
7. **S10e** (205 glosses ~ 6.03% of all glosses)
8. **S176** (193 glosses ~ 5.67% of all glosses)
9. **S192** (181 glosses ~ 5.32% of all glosses)
