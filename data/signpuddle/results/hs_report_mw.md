# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Malawi has a total of 48 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 45 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 84 total handshape tokens, of which 31
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 11 instances (making up 13.1% of all tokens) over 8 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (54.76%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 7 handshapes in the dataset. These top 7 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (11 tokens ~ 13.1%, 8 glosses)
2. **S14c** (8 tokens ~ 9.52%, 3 glosses)
3. **S100** (7 tokens ~ 8.33%, 6 glosses)
4. **S16d** (7 tokens ~ 8.33%, 5 glosses)
5. **S115** (5 tokens ~ 5.95%, 3 glosses)
6. **S113** (4 tokens ~ 4.76%, 1 glosses)
7. **S106** (4 tokens ~ 4.76%, 1 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 8 glosses
(16.67% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 7 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (8 glosses ~ 16.67% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (6 glosses ~ 12.5% of all glosses)
3. **S16d** (5 glosses ~ 10.42% of all glosses)
4. **S19a** (3 glosses ~ 6.25% of all glosses)
5. **S14c** (3 glosses ~ 6.25% of all glosses)
6. **S185** (3 glosses ~ 6.25% of all glosses)
7. **S115** (3 glosses ~ 6.25% of all glosses)

## Exclusions

### Glosses Without Handshape

Of the 48 total glosses, 6.25% (3 glosses) did not include any handshape
symbol (i.e. no FSW code starting with S1xx) and thus were excluded from
the handshape frequency analysis. These glosses are listed below.

- amen (sid_25)
- LOVE (sid_48)
- love (sid_1)
