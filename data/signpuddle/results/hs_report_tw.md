# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Taiwan has a total of 214 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 203 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 368 total handshape tokens, of which
63 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S1f5 with a
total of 52 instances (making up 14.13% of all tokens) over 39 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (54.62%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S1f5** (52 tokens ~ 14.13%, 39 glosses)
2. **S15a** (46 tokens ~ 12.5%, 34 glosses)
3. **S14c** (39 tokens ~ 10.6%, 29 glosses)
4. **S192** (23 tokens ~ 6.25%, 21 glosses)
5. **S100** (23 tokens ~ 6.25%, 21 glosses)
6. **S15d** (18 tokens ~ 4.89%, 7 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S1f5, appearing in 39 glosses
(18.22% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S1f5** (39 glosses ~ 18.22% of all glosses)
2. **S15a** (34 glosses ~ 15.89% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (29 glosses ~ 13.55% of all glosses)
4. **S100** (21 glosses ~ 9.81% of all glosses)
5. **S192** (21 glosses ~ 9.81% of all glosses)
6. **S10e** (10 glosses ~ 4.67% of all glosses)
