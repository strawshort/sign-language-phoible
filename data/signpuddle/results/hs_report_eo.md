# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Esperanto has a total of 2313 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 2281 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 4361 total handshape tokens, of
which 45 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 409 instances (making up 9.38% of all tokens) over 356
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.31%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 11 handshapes in the dataset. These top 11 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (409 tokens ~ 9.38%, 356 glosses)
2. **S14c** (269 tokens ~ 6.17%, 211 glosses)
3. **S100** (225 tokens ~ 5.16%, 166 glosses)
4. **S1ea** (189 tokens ~ 4.33%, 138 glosses)
5. **S18d** (169 tokens ~ 3.88%, 112 glosses)
6. **S1f7** (166 tokens ~ 3.81%, 117 glosses)
7. **S176** (162 tokens ~ 3.71%, 130 glosses)
8. **S140** (162 tokens ~ 3.71%, 116 glosses)
9. **S18c** (154 tokens ~ 3.53%, 106 glosses)
10. **S11a** (148 tokens ~ 3.39%, 110 glosses)
11. **S16d** (141 tokens ~ 3.23%, 100 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 356 glosses
(15.39% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 11 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (356 glosses ~ 15.39% of all glosses)
2. **S14c** (211 glosses ~ 9.12% of all glosses)
3. **S100** (166 glosses ~ 7.18% of all glosses)
4. **S1ea** (138 glosses ~ 5.97% of all glosses)
5. **S176** (130 glosses ~ 5.62% of all glosses)
