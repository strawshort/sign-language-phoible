# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Czech Republic has a total of 5830 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 5454 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 10595 total handshape
tokens, of which 122 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15d with a total of 1627 instances (making up 15.36% of
all tokens) over 1033 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.48%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (1627 tokens ~ 15.36%, 1033 glosses)
2. **S100** (1299 tokens ~ 12.26%, 840 glosses)
3. **S14c** (1021 tokens ~ 9.64%, 636 glosses)
4. **S115** (645 tokens ~ 6.09%, 365 glosses)
5. **S1ce** (473 tokens ~ 4.46%, 279 glosses)
6. **S1ea** (389 tokens ~ 3.67%, 259 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 1033
glosses (17.72% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6
handshapes that cover the most glosses, along with the number and
percent of glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (1033 glosses ~ 17.72% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (840 glosses ~ 14.41% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (636 glosses ~ 10.91% of all glosses)
4. **S115** (365 glosses ~ 6.26% of all glosses)
5. **S1ce** (279 glosses ~ 4.79% of all glosses)
6. **S1ea** (259 glosses ~ 4.44% of all glosses)
