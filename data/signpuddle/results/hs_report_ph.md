# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Philippines has a total of 855 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 814 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 1570 total handshape tokens, of which
85 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15d with a
total of 211 instances (making up 13.44% of all tokens) over 144
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (52.61%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 9 handshapes in the dataset. These top 9 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (211 tokens ~ 13.44%, 144 glosses)
2. **S100** (133 tokens ~ 8.47%, 100 glosses)
3. **S15a** (120 tokens ~ 7.64%, 81 glosses)
4. **S14c** (110 tokens ~ 7.01%, 72 glosses)
5. **S1f7** (70 tokens ~ 4.46%, 46 glosses)
6. **S115** (49 tokens ~ 3.12%, 35 glosses)
7. **S140** (47 tokens ~ 2.99%, 34 glosses)
8. **S176** (44 tokens ~ 2.8%, 35 glosses)
9. **S185** (42 tokens ~ 2.68%, 29 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 144 glosses
(16.84% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 9 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (144 glosses ~ 16.84% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (100 glosses ~ 11.7% of all glosses)
3. **S15a** (81 glosses ~ 9.47% of all glosses)
4. **S14c** (72 glosses ~ 8.42% of all glosses)
5. **S1f7** (46 glosses ~ 5.38% of all glosses)
6. **S115** (35 glosses ~ 4.09% of all glosses)
7. **S176** (35 glosses ~ 4.09% of all glosses)
8. **S140** (34 glosses ~ 3.98% of all glosses)
9. **S10e** (31 glosses ~ 3.63% of all glosses)
