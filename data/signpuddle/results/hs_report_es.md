# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Spain has a total of 8337 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 7989 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 14075 total handshape tokens, of
which 140 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 2957 instances (making up 21.01% of all tokens) over
1994 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.52%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (2957 tokens ~ 21.01%, 1994 glosses)
2. **S100** (1482 tokens ~ 10.53%, 1094 glosses)
3. **S14c** (1357 tokens ~ 9.64%, 876 glosses)
4. **S185** (585 tokens ~ 4.16%, 404 glosses)
5. **S1ea** (448 tokens ~ 3.18%, 334 glosses)
6. **S10e** (423 tokens ~ 3.01%, 332 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 1994
glosses (23.92% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6
handshapes that cover the most glosses, along with the number and
percent of glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (1994 glosses ~ 23.92% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (1094 glosses ~ 13.12% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (876 glosses ~ 10.51% of all glosses)
4. **S185** (404 glosses ~ 4.85% of all glosses)
5. **S1ea** (334 glosses ~ 4.01% of all glosses)
6. **S10e** (332 glosses ~ 3.98% of all glosses)
