# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

Dictionary, of which 3 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 4 total handshape tokens, of which 3
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S12d with a
total of 2 instances (making up 50.0% of all tokens) over 2 glosses.
