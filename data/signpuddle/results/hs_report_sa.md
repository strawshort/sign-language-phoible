# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Saudi Arabia has a total of 397 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 371 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 635 total handshape
tokens, of which 79 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15a with a total of 181 instances (making up 28.5% of all
tokens) over 116 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (52.28%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 4 handshapes in the dataset. These top 4 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (181 tokens ~ 28.5%, 116 glosses)
2. **S100** (66 tokens ~ 10.39%, 51 glosses)
3. **S14c** (45 tokens ~ 7.09%, 30 glosses)
4. **S177** (40 tokens ~ 6.3%, 27 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 116 glosses
(29.22% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 4 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (116 glosses ~ 29.22% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (51 glosses ~ 12.85% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (30 glosses ~ 7.56% of all glosses)
4. **S177** (27 glosses ~ 6.8% of all glosses)
