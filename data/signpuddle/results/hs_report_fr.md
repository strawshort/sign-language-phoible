# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for France has a total of 1299 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1218 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 1966 total handshape tokens, of
which 82 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15d
with a total of 276 instances (making up 14.04% of all tokens) over 198
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.56%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 7 handshapes in the dataset. These top 7 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (276 tokens ~ 14.04%, 198 glosses)
2. **S100** (184 tokens ~ 9.36%, 148 glosses)
3. **S14c** (160 tokens ~ 8.14%, 112 glosses)
4. **S15a** (133 tokens ~ 6.77%, 91 glosses)
5. **S185** (81 tokens ~ 4.12%, 53 glosses)
6. **S19a** (80 tokens ~ 4.07%, 64 glosses)
7. **S147** (80 tokens ~ 4.07%, 59 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 198 glosses
(15.24% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 7 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (198 glosses ~ 15.24% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (148 glosses ~ 11.39% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (112 glosses ~ 8.62% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (91 glosses ~ 7.01% of all glosses)
5. **S19a** (64 glosses ~ 4.93% of all glosses)
6. **S147** (59 glosses ~ 4.54% of all glosses)
7. **S185** (53 glosses ~ 4.08% of all glosses)
