# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for German Switzerland has a total of 4717 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 4464 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 8472 total handshape
tokens, of which 85 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15a with a total of 1086 instances (making up 12.82% of
all tokens) over 653 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.4%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 6 handshapes in the dataset. These top 6 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (1086 tokens ~ 12.82%, 653 glosses)
2. **S14c** (941 tokens ~ 11.11%, 589 glosses)
3. **S100** (771 tokens ~ 9.1%, 516 glosses)
4. **S15d** (658 tokens ~ 7.77%, 420 glosses)
5. **S1ea** (422 tokens ~ 4.98%, 293 glosses)
6. **S185** (392 tokens ~ 4.63%, 285 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 653 glosses
(13.84% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 6 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (653 glosses ~ 13.84% of all glosses)
2. **S14c** (589 glosses ~ 12.49% of all glosses)
3. **S100** (516 glosses ~ 10.94% of all glosses)
4. **S15d** (420 glosses ~ 8.9% of all glosses)
5. **S1ea** (293 glosses ~ 6.21% of all glosses)
6. **S185** (285 glosses ~ 6.04% of all glosses)
