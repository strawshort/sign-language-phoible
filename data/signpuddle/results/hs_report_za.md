# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for South Africa has a total of 154 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 152 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 262 total handshape
tokens, of which 63 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S100 with a total of 26 instances (making up 9.92% of all
tokens) over 20 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.76%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 9 handshapes in the dataset. These top 9 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S100** (26 tokens ~ 9.92%, 20 glosses)
2. **S15a** (23 tokens ~ 8.78%, 18 glosses)
3. **S14c** (22 tokens ~ 8.4%, 16 glosses)
4. **S15d** (22 tokens ~ 8.4%, 15 glosses)
5. **S115** (11 tokens ~ 4.2%, 7 glosses)
6. **S10e** (8 tokens ~ 3.05%, 7 glosses)
7. **S11a** (7 tokens ~ 2.67%, 3 glosses)
8. **S1f5** (7 tokens ~ 2.67%, 5 glosses)
9. **S1ea** (7 tokens ~ 2.67%, 3 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S100, appearing in 20 glosses
(12.99% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 9 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S100** (20 glosses ~ 12.99% of all glosses)
2. **S15a** (18 glosses ~ 11.69% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (16 glosses ~ 10.39% of all glosses)
4. **S15d** (15 glosses ~ 9.74% of all glosses)
5. **S115** (7 glosses ~ 4.55% of all glosses)
6. **S10e** (7 glosses ~ 4.55% of all glosses)
7. **S1f5** (5 glosses ~ 3.25% of all glosses)
8. **S147** (5 glosses ~ 3.25% of all glosses)
9. **S106** (5 glosses ~ 3.25% of all glosses)
