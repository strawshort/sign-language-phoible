# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for French Belgium has a total of 3465 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 3302 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 5706 total handshape
tokens, of which 105 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15d with a total of 738 instances (making up 12.93% of all
tokens) over 547 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.47%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 9 handshapes in the dataset. These top 9 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (738 tokens ~ 12.93%, 547 glosses)
2. **S14c** (474 tokens ~ 8.31%, 318 glosses)
3. **S100** (472 tokens ~ 8.27%, 326 glosses)
4. **S10e** (250 tokens ~ 4.38%, 182 glosses)
5. **S180** (218 tokens ~ 3.82%, 150 glosses)
6. **S16d** (192 tokens ~ 3.36%, 153 glosses)
7. **S1f5** (191 tokens ~ 3.35%, 144 glosses)
8. **S1d4** (178 tokens ~ 3.12%, 118 glosses)
9. **S19a** (167 tokens ~ 2.93%, 120 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 547 glosses
(15.79% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 9 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (547 glosses ~ 15.79% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (326 glosses ~ 9.41% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (318 glosses ~ 9.18% of all glosses)
4. **S10e** (182 glosses ~ 5.25% of all glosses)
5. **S16d** (153 glosses ~ 4.42% of all glosses)
6. **S180** (150 glosses ~ 4.33% of all glosses)
7. **S1f5** (144 glosses ~ 4.16% of all glosses)
8. **S19a** (120 glosses ~ 3.46% of all glosses)
9. **S185** (119 glosses ~ 3.43% of all glosses)
