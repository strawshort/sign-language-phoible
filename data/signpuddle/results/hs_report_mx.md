# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Mexico has a total of 1266 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1177 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 2040 total handshape tokens, of
which 122 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S14c
with a total of 207 instances (making up 10.15% of all tokens) over 145
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.25%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 11 handshapes in the dataset. These top 11 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S14c** (207 tokens ~ 10.15%, 145 glosses)
2. **S15d** (124 tokens ~ 6.08%, 89 glosses)
3. **S15a** (117 tokens ~ 5.74%, 81 glosses)
4. **S100** (95 tokens ~ 4.66%, 84 glosses)
5. **S16d** (86 tokens ~ 4.22%, 65 glosses)
6. **S1f5** (85 tokens ~ 4.17%, 62 glosses)
7. **S10e** (84 tokens ~ 4.12%, 64 glosses)
8. **S1dc** (66 tokens ~ 3.24%, 46 glosses)
9. **S192** (61 tokens ~ 2.99%, 46 glosses)
10. **S147** (50 tokens ~ 2.45%, 38 glosses)
11. **S140** (50 tokens ~ 2.45%, 46 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S14c, appearing in 145 glosses
(11.45% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 11 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S14c** (145 glosses ~ 11.45% of all glosses)
2. **S15d** (89 glosses ~ 7.03% of all glosses)
3. **S100** (84 glosses ~ 6.64% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (81 glosses ~ 6.4% of all glosses)
5. **S16d** (65 glosses ~ 5.13% of all glosses)
6. **S10e** (64 glosses ~ 5.06% of all glosses)
7. **S1f5** (62 glosses ~ 4.9% of all glosses)
8. **S140** (46 glosses ~ 3.63% of all glosses)
9. **S192** (46 glosses ~ 3.63% of all glosses)
10. **S1dc** (46 glosses ~ 3.63% of all glosses)
11. **S147** (38 glosses ~ 3.0% of all glosses)
