# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for French Switzerland has a total of 9580 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 8815 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 20211 total handshape
tokens, of which 149 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15d with a total of 2437 instances (making up 12.06% of
all tokens) over 1473 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.97%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 10 handshapes in the dataset. These top 10 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (2437 tokens ~ 12.06%, 1473 glosses)
2. **S100** (1669 tokens ~ 8.26%, 1000 glosses)
3. **S14c** (1659 tokens ~ 8.21%, 923 glosses)
4. **S10e** (853 tokens ~ 4.22%, 487 glosses)
5. **S15a** (772 tokens ~ 3.82%, 500 glosses)
6. **S185** (754 tokens ~ 3.73%, 446 glosses)
7. **S1ce** (650 tokens ~ 3.22%, 352 glosses)
8. **S1f5** (593 tokens ~ 2.93%, 373 glosses)
9. **S150** (568 tokens ~ 2.81%, 312 glosses)
10. **S16d** (549 tokens ~ 2.72%, 327 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 1473
glosses (15.38% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 10
handshapes that cover the most glosses, along with the number and
percent of glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (1473 glosses ~ 15.38% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (1000 glosses ~ 10.44% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (923 glosses ~ 9.63% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (500 glosses ~ 5.22% of all glosses)
5. **S10e** (487 glosses ~ 5.08% of all glosses)
6. **S185** (446 glosses ~ 4.66% of all glosses)
7. **S1f5** (373 glosses ~ 3.89% of all glosses)
8. **S1ce** (352 glosses ~ 3.67% of all glosses)
9. **S1ea** (328 glosses ~ 3.42% of all glosses)
10. **S16d** (327 glosses ~ 3.41% of all glosses)
