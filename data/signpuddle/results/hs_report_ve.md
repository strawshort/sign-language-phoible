# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Venezuela has a total of 134 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 130 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 216 total handshape tokens, of which
45 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 31 instances (making up 14.35% of all tokens) over 17 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.85%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 8 handshapes in the dataset. These top 8 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (31 tokens ~ 14.35%, 17 glosses)
2. **S100** (23 tokens ~ 10.65%, 17 glosses)
3. **S15d** (11 tokens ~ 5.09%, 6 glosses)
4. **S1ce** (11 tokens ~ 5.09%, 6 glosses)
5. **S1f7** (10 tokens ~ 4.63%, 5 glosses)
6. **S115** (9 tokens ~ 4.17%, 8 glosses)
7. **S176** (9 tokens ~ 4.17%, 7 glosses)
8. **S106** (8 tokens ~ 3.7%, 5 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 17 glosses
(12.69% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 8 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (17 glosses ~ 12.69% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (17 glosses ~ 12.69% of all glosses)
3. **S115** (8 glosses ~ 5.97% of all glosses)
4. **S1d4** (7 glosses ~ 5.22% of all glosses)
5. **S176** (7 glosses ~ 5.22% of all glosses)
6. **S1dc** (7 glosses ~ 5.22% of all glosses)
7. **S192** (6 glosses ~ 4.48% of all glosses)
8. **S14c** (6 glosses ~ 4.48% of all glosses)

## Exclusions

### Glosses Without Handshape

Of the 134 total glosses, 2.99% (4 glosses) did not include any
handshape symbol (i.e. no FSW code starting with S1xx) and thus were
excluded from the handshape frequency analysis. These glosses are listed
below.

- abuelo (grandfather) (sid_94)
- I-don- (sid_3)
- s (sid_34)
- si (yes) (sid_130)
