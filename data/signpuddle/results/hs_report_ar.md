# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Argentina has a total of 1868 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1719 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 2674 total handshape tokens, of
which 88 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 520 instances (making up 19.45% of all tokens) over 349
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (52.51%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 5 handshapes in the dataset. These top 5 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (520 tokens ~ 19.45%, 349 glosses)
2. **S100** (355 tokens ~ 13.28%, 275 glosses)
3. **S14c** (289 tokens ~ 10.81%, 192 glosses)
4. **S1ce** (131 tokens ~ 4.9%, 86 glosses)
5. **S10e** (109 tokens ~ 4.08%, 87 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 349 glosses
(18.68% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 5 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (349 glosses ~ 18.68% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (275 glosses ~ 14.72% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (192 glosses ~ 10.28% of all glosses)
4. **S10e** (87 glosses ~ 4.66% of all glosses)
5. **S1ce** (86 glosses ~ 4.6% of all glosses)
