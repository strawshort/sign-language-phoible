# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Malta has a total of 2753 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 2485 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 5392 total handshape tokens, of
which 130 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 580 instances (making up 10.76% of all tokens) over 371
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.72%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 8 handshapes in the dataset. These top 8 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (580 tokens ~ 10.76%, 371 glosses)
2. **S100** (564 tokens ~ 10.46%, 406 glosses)
3. **S14c** (527 tokens ~ 9.77%, 364 glosses)
4. **S15d** (485 tokens ~ 8.99%, 332 glosses)
5. **S1f7** (243 tokens ~ 4.51%, 166 glosses)
6. **S10e** (137 tokens ~ 2.54%, 123 glosses)
7. **S1ea** (127 tokens ~ 2.36%, 92 glosses)
8. **S150** (126 tokens ~ 2.34%, 81 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S100, appearing in 406 glosses
(14.75% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 8 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S100** (406 glosses ~ 14.75% of all glosses)
2. **S15a** (371 glosses ~ 13.48% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (364 glosses ~ 13.22% of all glosses)
4. **S15d** (332 glosses ~ 12.06% of all glosses)
5. **S1f7** (166 glosses ~ 6.03% of all glosses)
6. **S10e** (123 glosses ~ 4.47% of all glosses)
7. **S115** (101 glosses ~ 3.67% of all glosses)
8. **S1dc** (93 glosses ~ 3.38% of all glosses)
