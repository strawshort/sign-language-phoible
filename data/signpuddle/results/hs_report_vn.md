# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Vietnam has a total of 646 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 609 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 1041 total handshape tokens, of which
82 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 168 instances (making up 16.14% of all tokens) over 100
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (52.93%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 8 handshapes in the dataset. These top 8 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (168 tokens ~ 16.14%, 100 glosses)
2. **S100** (79 tokens ~ 7.59%, 58 glosses)
3. **S14c** (75 tokens ~ 7.2%, 61 glosses)
4. **S15d** (74 tokens ~ 7.11%, 55 glosses)
5. **S147** (45 tokens ~ 4.32%, 29 glosses)
6. **S1f5** (40 tokens ~ 3.84%, 28 glosses)
7. **S10e** (36 tokens ~ 3.46%, 31 glosses)
8. **S16d** (34 tokens ~ 3.27%, 18 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 100 glosses
(15.48% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 8 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (100 glosses ~ 15.48% of all glosses)
2. **S14c** (61 glosses ~ 9.44% of all glosses)
3. **S100** (58 glosses ~ 8.98% of all glosses)
4. **S15d** (55 glosses ~ 8.51% of all glosses)
5. **S10e** (31 glosses ~ 4.8% of all glosses)
6. **S147** (29 glosses ~ 4.49% of all glosses)
7. **S1f5** (28 glosses ~ 4.33% of all glosses)
8. **S1f7** (22 glosses ~ 3.41% of all glosses)
