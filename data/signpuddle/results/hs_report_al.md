# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Albania has a total of 130 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 124 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 213 total handshape tokens, of which
38 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S14c with a
total of 58 instances (making up 27.23% of all tokens) over 35 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (53.05%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 4 handshapes in the dataset. These top 4 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S14c** (58 tokens ~ 27.23%, 35 glosses)
2. **S100** (31 tokens ~ 14.55%, 24 glosses)
3. **S15d** (14 tokens ~ 6.57%, 12 glosses)
4. **S176** (10 tokens ~ 4.69%, 9 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S14c, appearing in 35 glosses
(26.92% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 4 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S14c** (35 glosses ~ 26.92% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (24 glosses ~ 18.46% of all glosses)
3. **S15d** (12 glosses ~ 9.23% of all glosses)
4. **S176** (9 glosses ~ 6.92% of all glosses)

## Exclusions

### Glosses Without Handshape

Of the 130 total glosses, 4.62% (6 glosses) did not include any
handshape symbol (i.e. no FSW code starting with S1xx) and thus were
excluded from the handshape frequency analysis. These glosses are listed
below.

- ? (sid_33)
- . (sid_34)
- Biçikletë (sid_73)
