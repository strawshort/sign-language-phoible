# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Flanders, Belgium has a total of 8788 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 8195 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 14377 total handshape
tokens, of which 137 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15d with a total of 2373 instances (making up 16.51% of
all tokens) over 1540 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (52.3%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 8 handshapes in the dataset. These top 8 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (2373 tokens ~ 16.51%, 1540 glosses)
2. **S14c** (1625 tokens ~ 11.3%, 1053 glosses)
3. **S100** (1401 tokens ~ 9.74%, 1054 glosses)
4. **S15a** (477 tokens ~ 3.32%, 367 glosses)
5. **S10e** (442 tokens ~ 3.07%, 352 glosses)
6. **S16d** (428 tokens ~ 2.98%, 305 glosses)
7. **S176** (390 tokens ~ 2.71%, 293 glosses)
8. **S1ea** (383 tokens ~ 2.66%, 284 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 1540
glosses (17.52% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 8
handshapes that cover the most glosses, along with the number and
percent of glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (1540 glosses ~ 17.52% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (1054 glosses ~ 11.99% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (1053 glosses ~ 11.98% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (367 glosses ~ 4.18% of all glosses)
5. **S10e** (352 glosses ~ 4.01% of all glosses)
6. **S16d** (305 glosses ~ 3.47% of all glosses)
7. **S176** (293 glosses ~ 3.33% of all glosses)
8. **S1ea** (284 glosses ~ 3.23% of all glosses)
