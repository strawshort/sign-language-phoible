# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Signs Used Worldwide has a total of 238 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 223 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 451 total handshape
tokens, of which 55 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15a with a total of 60 instances (making up 13.3% of all
tokens) over 33 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.88%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 8 handshapes in the dataset. These top 8 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (60 tokens ~ 13.3%, 33 glosses)
2. **S14c** (45 tokens ~ 9.98%, 27 glosses)
3. **S100** (36 tokens ~ 7.98%, 27 glosses)
4. **S15d** (23 tokens ~ 5.1%, 12 glosses)
5. **S115** (21 tokens ~ 4.66%, 14 glosses)
6. **S10e** (17 tokens ~ 3.77%, 13 glosses)
7. **S1f7** (17 tokens ~ 3.77%, 15 glosses)
8. **S1f5** (15 tokens ~ 3.33%, 12 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 33 glosses
(13.87% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 8 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (33 glosses ~ 13.87% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (27 glosses ~ 11.34% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (27 glosses ~ 11.34% of all glosses)
4. **S1f7** (15 glosses ~ 6.3% of all glosses)
5. **S115** (14 glosses ~ 5.88% of all glosses)
6. **S119** (13 glosses ~ 5.46% of all glosses)
7. **S10e** (13 glosses ~ 5.46% of all glosses)
8. **S15d** (12 glosses ~ 5.04% of all glosses)
