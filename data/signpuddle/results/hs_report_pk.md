# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Pakistan has a total of 13 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 12 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 20 total handshape tokens, of which 9
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 5 instances (making up 25.0% of all tokens) over 2 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (55.0%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 3 handshapes in the dataset. These top 3 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (5 tokens ~ 25.0%, 2 glosses)
2. **S14c** (3 tokens ~ 15.0%, 3 glosses)
3. **S100** (3 tokens ~ 15.0%, 2 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S14c, appearing in 3 glosses
(23.08% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 3 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S14c** (3 glosses ~ 23.08% of all glosses)
2. **S15a** (2 glosses ~ 15.38% of all glosses)
3. **S100** (2 glosses ~ 15.38% of all glosses)
