# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Tunisia has a total of 4511 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 3983 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 8631 total handshape tokens, of
which 165 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15d
with a total of 762 instances (making up 8.83% of all tokens) over 513
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.99%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 10 handshapes in the dataset. These top 10 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (762 tokens ~ 8.83%, 513 glosses)
2. **S14c** (748 tokens ~ 8.67%, 476 glosses)
3. **S100** (731 tokens ~ 8.47%, 494 glosses)
4. **S15a** (641 tokens ~ 7.43%, 378 glosses)
5. **S185** (358 tokens ~ 4.15%, 223 glosses)
6. **S10e** (319 tokens ~ 3.7%, 217 glosses)
7. **S1f5** (304 tokens ~ 3.52%, 202 glosses)
8. **S1ea** (258 tokens ~ 2.99%, 148 glosses)
9. **S1dc** (192 tokens ~ 2.22%, 124 glosses)
10. **S16d** (174 tokens ~ 2.02%, 117 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 513 glosses
(11.37% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 10 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (513 glosses ~ 11.37% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (494 glosses ~ 10.95% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (476 glosses ~ 10.55% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (378 glosses ~ 8.38% of all glosses)
5. **S185** (223 glosses ~ 4.94% of all glosses)
6. **S10e** (217 glosses ~ 4.81% of all glosses)
7. **S1f5** (202 glosses ~ 4.48% of all glosses)
8. **S1ea** (148 glosses ~ 3.28% of all glosses)
9. **S1dc** (124 glosses ~ 2.75% of all glosses)
10. **S16d** (117 glosses ~ 2.59% of all glosses)
