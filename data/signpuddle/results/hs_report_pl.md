# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Poland has a total of 2106 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 1998 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 3448 total handshape tokens, of
which 90 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15d
with a total of 542 instances (making up 15.72% of all tokens) over 361
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (52.03%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 5 handshapes in the dataset. These top 5 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (542 tokens ~ 15.72%, 361 glosses)
2. **S100** (460 tokens ~ 13.34%, 306 glosses)
3. **S14c** (408 tokens ~ 11.83%, 270 glosses)
4. **S15a** (215 tokens ~ 6.24%, 146 glosses)
5. **S1f5** (169 tokens ~ 4.9%, 130 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 361 glosses
(17.14% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 5 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (361 glosses ~ 17.14% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (306 glosses ~ 14.53% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (270 glosses ~ 12.82% of all glosses)
4. **S15a** (146 glosses ~ 6.93% of all glosses)
5. **S1f5** (130 glosses ~ 6.17% of all glosses)
