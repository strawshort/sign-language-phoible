# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Denmark has a total of 53 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 48 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 56 total handshape tokens, of which 33
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S100 with a
total of 10 instances (making up 17.86% of all tokens) over 5 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.79%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 10 handshapes in the dataset. These top 10 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S100** (10 tokens ~ 17.86%, 5 glosses)
2. **S1ed** (3 tokens ~ 5.36%, 2 glosses)
3. **S1f5** (2 tokens ~ 3.57%, 2 glosses)
4. **S101** (2 tokens ~ 3.57%, 2 glosses)
5. **S115** (2 tokens ~ 3.57%, 2 glosses)
6. **S1f0** (2 tokens ~ 3.57%, 2 glosses)
7. **S1eb** (2 tokens ~ 3.57%, 1 glosses)
8. **S1dc** (2 tokens ~ 3.57%, 2 glosses)
9. **S153** (2 tokens ~ 3.57%, 2 glosses)
10. **S176** (2 tokens ~ 3.57%, 2 glosses)

## Exclusions

### Glosses Without Handshape

Of the 53 total glosses, 9.43% (5 glosses) did not include any handshape
symbol (i.e. no FSW code starting with S1xx) and thus were excluded from
the handshape frequency analysis. These glosses are listed below.

- ◌́ (sid_42)
- ◌̈ (sid_43)
- Danish Sign Language Alphabet (sid_9)
- Danish Sign Language Numbers (sid_10)
- S (sid_31)
