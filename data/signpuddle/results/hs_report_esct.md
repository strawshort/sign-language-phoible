# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Catalonia, Barcelona has a total of 3380 glosses in the
SignPuddle Dictionary, of which 3256 contain handshapes (i.e., include
S1xx in their FSW codes). This dataset contains 6336 total handshape
tokens, of which 126 are unique handshapes. The most widely used
handshape is S15a with a total of 778 instances (making up 12.28% of all
tokens) over 502 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.88%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 8 handshapes in the dataset. These top 8 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (778 tokens ~ 12.28%, 502 glosses)
2. **S14c** (561 tokens ~ 8.85%, 364 glosses)
3. **S15d** (549 tokens ~ 8.66%, 371 glosses)
4. **S100** (511 tokens ~ 8.07%, 370 glosses)
5. **S1ea** (268 tokens ~ 4.23%, 187 glosses)
6. **S10e** (226 tokens ~ 3.57%, 185 glosses)
7. **S185** (210 tokens ~ 3.31%, 144 glosses)
8. **S115** (184 tokens ~ 2.9%, 107 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 502 glosses
(14.85% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 8 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (502 glosses ~ 14.85% of all glosses)
2. **S15d** (371 glosses ~ 10.98% of all glosses)
3. **S100** (370 glosses ~ 10.95% of all glosses)
4. **S14c** (364 glosses ~ 10.77% of all glosses)
5. **S1ea** (187 glosses ~ 5.53% of all glosses)
6. **S10e** (185 glosses ~ 5.47% of all glosses)
7. **S185** (144 glosses ~ 4.26% of all glosses)
8. **S1f5** (128 glosses ~ 3.79% of all glosses)
