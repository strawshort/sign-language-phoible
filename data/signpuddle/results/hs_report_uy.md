# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Uruguay has a total of 272 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 266 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 405 total handshape tokens, of which
72 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15d with a
total of 54 instances (making up 13.33% of all tokens) over 41 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.36%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 8 handshapes in the dataset. These top 8 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (54 tokens ~ 13.33%, 41 glosses)
2. **S14c** (40 tokens ~ 9.88%, 32 glosses)
3. **S100** (27 tokens ~ 6.67%, 22 glosses)
4. **S11e** (24 tokens ~ 5.93%, 21 glosses)
5. **S115** (21 tokens ~ 5.19%, 14 glosses)
6. **S15a** (16 tokens ~ 3.95%, 13 glosses)
7. **S10e** (14 tokens ~ 3.46%, 12 glosses)
8. **S177** (12 tokens ~ 2.96%, 10 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 41 glosses
(15.07% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 8 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (41 glosses ~ 15.07% of all glosses)
2. **S14c** (32 glosses ~ 11.76% of all glosses)
3. **S100** (22 glosses ~ 8.09% of all glosses)
4. **S11e** (21 glosses ~ 7.72% of all glosses)
5. **S115** (14 glosses ~ 5.15% of all glosses)
6. **S15a** (13 glosses ~ 4.78% of all glosses)
7. **S10e** (12 glosses ~ 4.41% of all glosses)
8. **S147** (11 glosses ~ 4.04% of all glosses)
