# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Bolivia has a total of 5 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 5 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 9 total handshape tokens, of which 6
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 3 instances (making up 33.33% of all tokens) over 2 glosses.
