# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Thailand has a total of 665 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 574 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 1276 total handshape tokens, of which
95 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 161 instances (making up 12.62% of all tokens) over 90 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.41%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 7 handshapes in the dataset. These top 7 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (161 tokens ~ 12.62%, 90 glosses)
2. **S100** (135 tokens ~ 10.58%, 111 glosses)
3. **S15d** (131 tokens ~ 10.27%, 78 glosses)
4. **S14c** (90 tokens ~ 7.05%, 66 glosses)
5. **S1f4** (69 tokens ~ 5.41%, 54 glosses)
6. **S115** (36 tokens ~ 2.82%, 26 glosses)
7. **S185** (34 tokens ~ 2.66%, 24 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S100, appearing in 111 glosses
(16.69% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 7 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S100** (111 glosses ~ 16.69% of all glosses)
2. **S15a** (90 glosses ~ 13.53% of all glosses)
3. **S15d** (78 glosses ~ 11.73% of all glosses)
4. **S14c** (66 glosses ~ 9.92% of all glosses)
5. **S1f4** (54 glosses ~ 8.12% of all glosses)
6. **S10e** (30 glosses ~ 4.51% of all glosses)
7. **S115** (26 glosses ~ 3.91% of all glosses)
