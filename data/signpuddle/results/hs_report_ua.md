# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Ukraine has a total of 37 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 36 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 39 total handshape tokens, of which 27
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S1ea with a
total of 4 instances (making up 10.26% of all tokens) over 2 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.28%) of all handshape
