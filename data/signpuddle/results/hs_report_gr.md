# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Greece has a total of 24 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 24 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 24 total handshape tokens, of which 18
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S115 with a
total of 3 instances (making up 12.5% of all tokens) over 3 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (54.17%) of all handshape
