# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Nigeria has a total of 10 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 8 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in their
FSW codes). This dataset contains 16 total handshape tokens, of which 6
are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a with a
total of 5 instances (making up 31.25% of all tokens) over 2 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (56.25%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 2 handshapes in the dataset. These top 2 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (5 tokens ~ 31.25%, 2 glosses)
2. **S14c** (4 tokens ~ 25.0%, 2 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 2 glosses
(20.0% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 2 handshapes
that cover the most glosses, along with the number and percent of
glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (2 glosses ~ 20.0% of all glosses)
2. **S14c** (2 glosses ~ 20.0% of all glosses)

## Exclusions

### Glosses Without Handshape

Of the 10 total glosses, 20.0% (2 glosses) did not include any handshape
symbol (i.e. no FSW code starting with S1xx) and thus were excluded from
the handshape frequency analysis. These glosses are listed below.

- Beginners sign (sid_4)
- God is with us (sid_9)
