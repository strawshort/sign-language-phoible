# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Canada has a total of 12041 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 11292 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 26042 total handshape tokens, of
which 159 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15d
with a total of 4161 instances (making up 15.98% of all tokens) over
2511 glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (50.27%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 9 handshapes in the dataset. These top 9 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15d** (4161 tokens ~ 15.98%, 2511 glosses)
2. **S100** (2236 tokens ~ 8.59%, 1513 glosses)
3. **S14c** (1844 tokens ~ 7.08%, 1134 glosses)
4. **S10e** (958 tokens ~ 3.68%, 648 glosses)
5. **S180** (943 tokens ~ 3.62%, 557 glosses)
6. **S16d** (935 tokens ~ 3.59%, 631 glosses)
7. **S185** (878 tokens ~ 3.37%, 570 glosses)
8. **S147** (570 tokens ~ 2.19%, 339 glosses)
9. **S1ce** (567 tokens ~ 2.18%, 331 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15d, appearing in 2511
glosses (20.85% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 9
handshapes that cover the most glosses, along with the number and
percent of glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15d** (2511 glosses ~ 20.85% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (1513 glosses ~ 12.57% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (1134 glosses ~ 9.42% of all glosses)
4. **S10e** (648 glosses ~ 5.38% of all glosses)
5. **S16d** (631 glosses ~ 5.24% of all glosses)
6. **S185** (570 glosses ~ 4.73% of all glosses)
7. **S180** (557 glosses ~ 4.63% of all glosses)
8. **S1f7** (372 glosses ~ 3.09% of all glosses)
9. **S147** (339 glosses ~ 2.82% of all glosses)
