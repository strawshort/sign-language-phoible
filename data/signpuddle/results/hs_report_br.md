# Handshape Analysis Summary

## Overview

The dataset for Brazil has a total of 54593 glosses in the SignPuddle
Dictionary, of which 48458 contain handshapes (i.e., include S1xx in
their FSW codes). This dataset contains 98046 total handshape tokens, of
which 247 are unique handshapes. The most widely used handshape is S15a
with a total of 8562 instances (making up 8.73% of all tokens) over 5954
glosses.

### Handshape Use by Token Count

A Pareto analysis shows that more than half (51.99%) of all handshape
tokens correspond to just 12 handshapes in the dataset. These top 12 are
shown below with their token count, % use, and gloss coverage.

1. **S15a** (8562 tokens ~ 8.73%, 5954 glosses)
2. **S100** (7013 tokens ~ 7.15%, 5386 glosses)
3. **S14c** (6988 tokens ~ 7.13%, 4970 glosses)
4. **S15d** (4045 tokens ~ 4.13%, 2752 glosses)
5. **S1dc** (3780 tokens ~ 3.86%, 3095 glosses)
6. **S1f7** (3779 tokens ~ 3.85%, 2674 glosses)
7. **S176** (3506 tokens ~ 3.58%, 2721 glosses)
8. **S16d** (3224 tokens ~ 3.29%, 2634 glosses)
9. **S10e** (3052 tokens ~ 3.11%, 2512 glosses)
10. **S192** (2560 tokens ~ 2.61%, 2005 glosses)
11. **S115** (2326 tokens ~ 2.37%, 1832 glosses)
12. **S11a** (2142 tokens ~ 2.18%, 1817 glosses)

### Handshape Use by Gloss Coverage

The most used handshape across glosses is S15a, appearing in 5954
glosses (10.91% of all glosses in the dataset). Below are the top 12
handshapes that cover the most glosses, along with the number and
percent of glosses that include the handshape at least once.

1. **S15a** (5954 glosses ~ 10.91% of all glosses)
2. **S100** (5386 glosses ~ 9.87% of all glosses)
3. **S14c** (4970 glosses ~ 9.1% of all glosses)
4. **S1dc** (3095 glosses ~ 5.67% of all glosses)
5. **S15d** (2752 glosses ~ 5.04% of all glosses)
6. **S176** (2721 glosses ~ 4.98% of all glosses)
7. **S1f7** (2674 glosses ~ 4.9% of all glosses)
8. **S16d** (2634 glosses ~ 4.82% of all glosses)
9. **S10e** (2512 glosses ~ 4.6% of all glosses)
10. **S192** (2005 glosses ~ 3.67% of all glosses)
